Pixelcade Companion for iPhone, iPad and Android

The Pixelcade app finds your Pixelcade LED and LCD marquees automatically and opens Pixelcade Companion for each one, where you control artwork, widgets and settings.

  • Finds every Pixelcade on your WiFi, no IP addresses needed
  • One card per cabinet: its LED and LCD marquees together
  • Opens Pixelcade Companion for each device
  • Sets up a new Pixelcade LCD on your WiFi
  • Add a Pixelcade by IP address when your network hides it
Your phone or tablet needs to be on the same WiFi network as your Pixelcade.

Most PCs need the 64-bit version. Choose ARM only for ARM-based Windows laptops (e.g. Snapdragon). Unzip it and run Pixelcade Companion.exe.
