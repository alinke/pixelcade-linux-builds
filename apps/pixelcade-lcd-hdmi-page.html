Pixelcade LCD HDMI Mode

Run your Pixelcade LCD marquee straight from your arcade computer's second HDMI port. No separate single board computer inside the marquee: the Pixelcade LCD software runs on the same computer as your games. Free for Pixelcade LCDs bought directly from Pixelcade.

Windows 64-bit Batocera x86-64 PC Batocera Raspberry Pi 5

How it works

A Pixelcade LCD normally has its own single board computer that drives the marquee screen. In LCD HDMI Mode the LCD bar plugs into your arcade computer's second HDMI output instead. Pixelcade downloads the LCD software, starts it with your computer, keeps it updated and shows your marquees on the bar, while your games play on the main screen.

What LCD HDMI Mode is

Why use it

One computer

No extra single board computer in the marquee to power, network or update. Everything runs on the computer you already have.

The full LCD experience

Game and console marquees, videos, scrolling text, achievements and widgets, the same as a Pixelcade LCD with its own board.

Works with your apps

Your front end, Pixelcade Companion and the Pixelcade phone apps all work as before. The LCD shows up next to your LED in the apps.

Supported systems

LCD HDMI Mode currently runs on Windows and on Batocera. It has been tested on a Raspberry Pi 5 and on a PC with two HDMI ports.

Other systems, such as other Linux arcade distributions and 32-bit computers, aren't supported yet. On those, use a Pixelcade LCD with its own board.

Getting started

Connect the bar

Plug the Pixelcade LCD bar into your computer's second HDMI port, and the game screen into the first.

Turn it on

Batocera: the Pixelcade installer offers LCD HDMI Mode when it finds an LCD bar. Windows: in Pixelcade Companion, go to Settings > Pixelcade LCD > LCD HDMI Mode.

Let the artwork download

The first time, the LCD downloads its Artwork Library, about 20 GB. Artwork for the games on your computer comes first, and the bar shows the progress.

Artwork on your arcade computer. The Pixelcade LCD artwork is downloaded to your arcade computer during installation, and new artwork keeps arriving with ongoing updates. Manage the artwork and its updates from the Pixelcade Companion app for iOS or Android.

Your marquees work during the download: anything already downloaded shows right away, and the rest fills in as it arrives. Pixelcade Companion shows the download's progress on its home page.

Free with a Pixelcade LCD

Bought your Pixelcade LCD directly from Pixelcade? The LCD HDMI Mode software is free for you.

Otherwise, LCD HDMI Mode starts with a 14-day free trial with everything turned on. After the trial, without a license, the LCD keeps showing the Pixelcade logo and console marquees; game marquees, text, achievements and widgets need a license. You can unlock it from the trial screen on the LCD or in Pixelcade Companion.

What you need

  • A Pixelcade LCD marquee bar
  • An arcade computer with two HDMI outputs, running 64-bit Windows or Batocera (x86-64 PC or Raspberry Pi 5)
  • About 20 GB of free disk space for the Artwork Library
  • An Internet connection for the first download and updates
