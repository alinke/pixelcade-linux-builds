Pixelcade Sidekick — test firmware

Test build for one board only. This is for the older 10.1″ Guition, whose label on the back says SKU: 10153001 and Models: JC8012P4A1C_I_W_Y (no “1” at the end). If your label says 10153002-V2 / JC8012P4A1C_I_W_Y1, use the normal flasher instead.
Please try these in order, A to D, and note what you see with each. Flash each one through the bottom USB port labeled UART (Chrome or Edge on a desktop computer). For B, C and D, use the top USB port afterwards, as usual. For each one, a photo at low and at full brightness helps, plus: are the colors washed out? Any fine vertical lines? Any flicker?

A — Guition’s own factory firmware (for comparison)

Guition factory demo, old panel (V3.3)

Guition’s own firmware for this panel, not Pixelcade’s — it shows Guition’s demo screens. It tells us whether the lines/washed-out look come from the panel itself or from our firmware. About 14 MB, so it takes a few minutes to flash.

B — Pixelcade test firmware

Pixelcade Sidekick 10.1″ (Guition V1) — test B

Our firmware with the old panel’s settings, sent in the same order as Guition’s own firmware. The screen shows “Pixelcade Sidekick 10.1″ (Guition V1)” when it starts.

C — B with a slower backlight signal

Pixelcade Sidekick 10.1″ (Guition V1) — test C

Same as B, but the backlight dimming signal runs at 5 kHz instead of 44 kHz. Compare low brightness with B: is the flicker gone, are the colors less dim?

D — B with a faster screen refresh

Pixelcade Sidekick 10.1″ (Guition V1) — test D

Same as B, but the screen refreshes about 62 times a second instead of 52. Compare full brightness with B: is the flicker gone? If the picture breaks up or goes black, that’s useful to know too — just flash B again.
